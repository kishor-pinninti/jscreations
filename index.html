How a custom piece comes together

Matching a saree, recreating a family design, or planning a bridal set: start with a message.

  1. Share your idea

    Send a photo, a saree swatch or a description on WhatsApp.

  2. Choose stones & finish

    Pick kemp, pachi, pearl or crystal, and a matte or bright gold tone.

  3. Handcrafted

    Your piece is assembled and finished by hand. Timelines are confirmed when you order.

  4. Delivered to you

    Packed with care and sent to your door, or picked up in person.

About

The hands behind JS Creations

JS Creations began with a love for the jewellery we grew up seeing at temple festivals and family weddings: Lakshmi coin pendants, peacock motifs, lotus jhumkas and strings of pearls.

Prasanna designs and makes each piece by hand, choosing every stone and bead so the finished jewellery feels like an heirloom without the heirloom price.

She works from Vizianagaram in Andhra Pradesh and takes orders over WhatsApp, for local pickup or delivery.

With love, Prasanna
